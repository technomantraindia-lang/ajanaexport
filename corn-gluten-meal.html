About the Product

Superior Corn Gluten Meal

Corn Gluten Meal is a high-protein feed ingredient obtained during the corn wet milling process. It is widely used in poultry, cattle, aqua, and pet food industries due to its rich protein content, good digestibility, and high energy value. Corn Gluten Meal is also known for its natural yellow pigment, which helps improve the color of poultry feed and supports better pigmentation in egg yolk and broiler skin.

Ajana Impex supplies quality Corn Gluten Meal in India for feed manufacturers looking for a reliable and nutritious protein source. The product is carefully sourced and checked to ensure consistent quality and safe usage in animal feed formulations.
