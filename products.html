Balanced Nutrition For Livestock

Balanced animal feed should include appropriate levels of protein, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water. Protein aids growth and tissue repair, while carbohydrates provide energy. Fats serve as concentrated energy sources and aid in vitamin absorption.

Properly balanced feed supports optimal health, growth, and performance in animals, ensuring they receive all necessary nutrients for their species-specific needs and activities.
