About the Product

Efficient Deoiled Corn DDGS

Deoiled Corn DDGS is a valuable co-product obtained from corn-based ethanol production. It is widely used as a cost-effective and nutritious feed ingredient for cattle, poultry, aqua, and layer feed. Due to its good protein level, energy value, and digestibility, it is considered a suitable alternative to conventional feed ingredients such as soybean meal and corn.

Ajana Impex is recognized as a reliable manufacturer and supplier in India, focusing on providing consistent, nutrient-rich ingredients that support better animal performance and feed efficiency.
