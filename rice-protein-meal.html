About the Product

Superior Rice Protein Meal

Rice Protein Meal is a valuable by-product produced during the wet milling of rice and starch extraction process. It is widely recognized as a highly digestible, sustainable, and efficient protein source for animal nutrition. Due to its excellent nutritional profile, it is increasingly being used as a strong alternative to soybean meal in livestock, poultry, aqua, and pet feed formulations.

With low aflatoxin levels, rich amino acid content, and a clean appearance, it offers better digestibility and consistent quality for feed manufacturers globally.
