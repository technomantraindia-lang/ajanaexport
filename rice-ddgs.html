About the Product

Premium Rice DDGS

Ajana Impex positions itself as the leading supplier of Rice DDGS across India. Rice DDGS is a byproduct of distilleries in which rice is used in the ethanol industry as a raw material. The high energy and good digestible protein in Rice DDGS makes it an ideal protein alternative to traditional sources of corn (energy) and soya meal (protein) in poultry, cattle, and aqua industries.
