About the Product

Versatile Corn Feed Meal

Corn Feed Meal is a nutritious and economical feed ingredient derived from the corn processing industry. It is commonly used in cattle, poultry, aqua, and livestock feed due to its balanced nutritional value, good palatability, and easy availability. Corn Feed Meal provides energy, protein, and fiber, making it a useful ingredient in various feed formulations.

Ajana Impex offers quality Corn Feed Meal in India for feed manufacturers and livestock nutrition companies, ensuring consistent quality, proper texture, and dependable nutritional value.
