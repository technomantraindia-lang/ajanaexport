About the Product

Benchmark Soyabean Meal

Soyabean Meal is one of the most widely used protein sources in poultry, livestock, and aqua feed industries. It is obtained as a by-product after the extraction of oil from soyabean seeds. Due to its high protein content, good amino acid profile, and excellent palatability, Soyabean Meal is considered a standard protein ingredient in animal nutrition.

It is highly digestible and provides essential nutrients that support growth, muscle development, milk production, and overall animal health across cattle, poultry, pigs, fish, sheep, and horses.
