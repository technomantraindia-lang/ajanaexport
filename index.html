Welcome to Ajana Impex Pvt. Ltd.

Ajana Impex Pvt. Ltd. is a reliable manufacturer, supplier, and exporter of quality animal feed ingredients and feed supplements based in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. Established in January 2012, the company has built strong experience in serving farmers, feed manufacturers, livestock businesses, poultry businesses, aqua feed industries, and global buyers.

Founded by Mr. Mahipat Ajana, Ajana Impex focuses on delivering dependable feed products with consistent quality, practical pricing, and reliable supply support. With more than 12 years of experience, the company has developed a trusted presence in the animal feed industry across India and international markets.

The company supplies a wide range of feed ingredients including Corn DDGS, Rice DDGS, Rice Protein Meal, Deoiled Corn DDGS, Corn Gluten Meal, Corn Feed Meal, Soyabean Meal, Meat Bone Meal, Poultry Meal, Fish Meal, Rice Gluten Meal, Rapeseed DOC, Sunflower DOC, Guar Korma, DORB, and other feed supplements.

Ajana Impex serves customers in more than 25 countries with a strong supply chain, export-ready packaging, timely coordination, and customer-focused service.
